The syllabus

One business.
Four weeks.

Start with an idea you can test online, or bring a product you’ve already started. You’ll work on the same business each week.

Week 01

What to hill climb?

Pick a customer, a problem, and one number worth improving. If people visit your product but nobody tries it, what would you change first? Design a test small enough to run this week.

You’ll leave with A one-page experiment and a baseline.

Week 02

Ship something I can try.

Build the smallest useful version of your product. Compare a hosted builder, a static site, and an app with a database. Deploy yours, then watch a cohort mate try it without instructions.

You’ll leave with A working link and a fix based on peer feedback.

Week 03

Reach the next customer.

Choose a channel where your customers already spend time. We’ll look at X, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Build a workflow for one channel that drafts, queues for review, and tracks what brings someone back to your product.

You’ll leave with A distribution workflow and a way to measure response.

Week 04

Give the business a runbook.

Start with the inbox. Teach an agent to sort a support request, draft a reply, and flag the cases you should handle. Turn a correction into a rule, then replay the case to see if the new rule works. Use the same process for the next recurring task.

You’ll leave with A tested support workflow and your first operating rules.
