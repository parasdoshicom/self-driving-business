The syllabus

Inside the workshop

A first sale in four weeks is the goal. Whether someone buys is up to them. We’ll work through every step together, including what to change when an offer gets no takers. Each week you’ll show the group what you tried and hear what happened when they tried it.

Week 01

What to hill climb?

Start with a few problems you’ve seen people spend time or money on. Use AI to sketch different offers, then talk to people who might buy. What do they do about the problem today? What would they pay to do differently? Use their answers to pick your next test. You can switch hills.

Week 02

Hand someone the link.

Give your most promising idea a price and a way to buy. We’ll compare hosted builders, static sites, and apps with a database, then put a small digital product online. Hand the link to a cohort mate and watch them try it. Can they tell what they’re buying? Can they check out?

Week 03

Where would your first customer look?

Find a place where people already talk about the problem. That might be Reddit, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok. Make an offer there and ask for the sale. Build a weekly routine for drafting and reviewing posts, then follow the clicks through to checkout. If people look but nobody buys, take that back to the group.

Week 04

Who answers the inbox?

Give your agent two support emails. One customer lost their download link. Another wants a refund for a reason you’ve never seen. Decide what the agent can handle and when it should come get you. Write down the rule and give a cohort mate a fresh case to test it. We’ll close by comparing what sold, what people passed on, and what each of us would try next.
