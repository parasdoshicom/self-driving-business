The syllabus

Inside the workshop

Come with a rough idea or something you’ve already shipped. Each week we’ll open up our work for the group. If you figure something out, show the rest of us how you did it.

Week 01

What to hill climb?

Say ten people try your product and nobody comes back. I’d want to know where the first person got stuck before asking AI to build another feature. Bring a customer problem. We’ll pick one thing worth improving and work out how you’d know it got better.

Week 02

Hand someone the link.

Get a first version online. We’ll look at when a hosted builder is enough, when a static site will do, and when you actually need a database. Then hand the link to someone in the cohort. Watch where they click before you explain anything. Fix the first thing that trips them up.

Week 03

Where would your first customer look?

Maybe it’s a Reddit thread where people already ask about your problem. Maybe it’s a two-minute YouTube demo. We’ll compare those with X, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok. Pick one channel and build a routine you can run again next week. AI drafts the post, you approve it, and a tracked link tells you whether anyone came over to try the product.

Week 04

Who answers the inbox?

Give your agent two support emails. One customer lost their download link. Another wants a refund for a reason you’ve never seen. Decide what the agent can handle and when it should come get you. Write down the rule and give a cohort mate a fresh case to test it. Then choose the next job in your business that could use the same treatment.
